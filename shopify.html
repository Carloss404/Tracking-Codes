Description 

Grape essence is a concentrated aromatic compound designed to replicate the sweet, tangy, and floral notes of fresh grapes. Formulated with natural extracts and key aroma compounds like methyl anthranilate and ethyl anthranilate, it is available in liquid form. Widely used in beverages, cosmetics, food, and health products, it delivers authentic grape flavor and aroma with versatile applications.

      Comparing Natural and Synthetic Options

      Natural grape essence is derived from grape juice or skins, often certified Kosher or organic, offering a full-bodied and authentic aroma. Synthetic varieties provide a cost-effective solution with a simplified aroma profile, making them ideal for industrial applications without compromising reliability.
